Footballer Draft

Two players and a host. Bid for footballers or race to name them. The stage is what gets filmed.

If voice bids stop working while recording on your phone, untick this.

Game setup

Changing the mode, budget, pool, challenge or team starts a new game.

Auction desk

Just say the number, like "3" or "3 dollars". Each new bid goes to the other player, so you don't need to say your name, but you can ("Tom 5").

Secret bids: each types a bid without the other looking

Name race desk

Take turns saying a player's name out loud. The game recognises him, adds his number to your score and passes the turn.

Player photos

Find photos on Google

On Google, press and hold a photo, tap Copy, come back and paste it in the box. Photos stay saved on this phone, so you only add each player once. Leave the name empty to use the player on the card.

How to play

Heights, ages and ratings come from a built-in list of about 190 players and are approximate. Anyone not on the list can still be added.
