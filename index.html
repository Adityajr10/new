Footballer Draft

Two players and a host. Bid for footballers or race to name them. The stage is what gets filmed.

Game setup

Changing the mode, budget, pool, challenge or team starts a new game.

Auction desk

Just say the number, like "3" or "3 dollars". Each new bid goes to the other player, so you don't need to say your name, but you can ("Tom 5"). No mic? Tap your half of the stage to raise the bid by $1.

Secret bids: each types a bid without the other looking

How to play

Heights, ages and ratings come from a built-in list of about 190 players and are approximate. Anyone not on the list can still be added.
